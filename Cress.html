Description

This is a work-in-progress sculpt of a character named Cress from the RPG Tales of Phantasia. The sculpt is being created using ZBrush, and the focus is on capturing the character's unique features and personality. The images showcase different angles of the sculpt, highlighting the details and craftsmanship involved in bringing Cress to life. The project is ongoing, and further refinements and textures will be added as development continues.
